## 1、The Broken Robot (损坏的机器人)
Leo wanted his robot, "Titan," to win the school battle bot contest. Titan was big and had a strong metal arm for fighting.
During the final match, disaster struck. The enemy robot hit Titan hard, and Titan’s metal arm broke. It couldn't attack anymore. The crowd gasped.
Leo had to act fast. He could give up, or he could change his plan.
Leo chose to use speed instead of power. He used the remote control to make Titan drive very fast in circles around the enemy.
The enemy robot got confused. It tried to hit the fast-moving Titan but missed, lost its balance, and crashed heavily into the wall.
The enemy robot broke down. Leo won the match! He proved that being smart and fast was just as important as being strong.

## 2、The Shadow Map
Dan walked proudly in front. His goal was to lead his little brother, Ben, to the secret lake before sunset. He held the paper map tightly.
Suddenly, a strong wind rushed through the trees. The map slipped from Dan’s hand and fell into a fast river. The water carried it away in seconds. Ben’s eyes filled with tears. The forest looked dark and scary. They were lost.
Dan’s stomach dropped. He wanted to shout and panic. But he looked at Ben's scared face. He had to choose: act like a helpless kid, or act like a responsible big brother.
Dan took a deep breath. "Don't worry," he said softly. He chose to trust his memory and nature. He remembered the lake was to the east.
He looked up at the sky. The sun was going down, turning the clouds orange. "The sun sets in the west," Dan thought. He turned his back to the sun. His shadow stretched out straight in front of him. "Follow my shadow," he told Ben.
They walked for twenty minutes. The trees opened up. The beautiful, quiet lake was right there. Dan smiled, realizing that a calm mind is the best map of all.

## 3、分集故事样例
Part 1: The Strange Signal
Theme: The Hook & Initial Mystery
Leo sat at his desk, staring at his old laptop. It was 11:00 PM. The house was quiet. His goal was just to finish his physics homework.
Suddenly, the screen went completely black. A single line of green text appeared: WARNING: PROTOCOL 7 ACTIVATED.
Leo frowned. He tried to close the window, but the mouse wouldn't move. A file folder popped open automatically. It was a video file named DoNotOpen.mp4.
His heart beat faster. He had a choice. He could force-shut down the computer, or he could click play.
Curiosity won. Leo clicked the mouse.
The video showed a dimly lit room that looked exactly like his father’s locked home office downstairs. A man in a dark hoodie sat in the chair, speaking directly into the camera.
"Leo," the man said in a low, distorted voice. "I know you are watching. You have ten minutes to find the silver flash drive in your dad's desk. If you don't, the security system in this house will lock down forever."
Before Leo could think, the video cut to black. The bedroom door suddenly clicked shut. Clunk. The smart lock was engaged. He was trapped inside his own room.

Part 2: The Locked Door
Theme: Escalating Stakes & Quick Thinking
Leo rushed to the door and twisted the handle. It didn't budge. He pulled hard, but the smart lock held fast.
His phone buzzed on the desk. A new text message from an unknown number: Tick-tock, Leo. 8 minutes left. The flash drive holds the master code to your house.
The crisis just grew bigger. He wasn't just locked in; the entire house network was under attack. If the mysterious person locked the house, his parents downstairs wouldn't be able to get in, and he couldn't get out.
Leo had to act. He looked around his bedroom. There was no flash drive here. But wait—the video showed his dad's office. How could he get out?
He spotted a loose air vent near the floor. It was small, but a 14-year-old could squeeze through it if he took off his jacket.
Leo dropped to his knees. He grabbed a screwdriver from his toolbox and started unscrewing the metal grate. Screeech. The screws came out one by one.
He pulled the grate off and looked into the dark, dusty ventilation shaft. It led straight down into the hallway outside his bedroom.
He crawled inside, closing his eyes against the dust, and started to wiggle forward into the dark.

Part 3: The Vent Trap
Theme: Physical Danger & The Midpoint Turn
Leo crawled through the narrow metal pipe. It was pitch black, and the air smelled like old dust.
Scratch. Scratch.
A strange sound echoed from the pipe ahead of him. Leo froze. He pressed his back against the cold metal wall. His heart hammered in his chest. Was there an animal? Or was someone else inside the ventilation system?
He turned on his phone flashlight. The beam cut through the dark dust. Ten feet ahead, blocking the path, was a small, glowing red camera lens, mounted on a robotic crawler. It was scanning the pipe.
If the camera saw him, the system would lock the vent shut, trapping him forever.
He had a choice. He could try to crawl back to his room, or he could slide past the camera when it turned away.
Leo waited, holding his breath. The camera turned left.
Now! Leo pushed forward with all his strength, sliding fast across the metal floor.
Click. The camera spun back instantly. A bright red laser beam flashed across Leo’s jacket.
ALARM TRIGGERED, a robotic voice echoed through the pipes. The metal grate behind him slammed shut with a loud bang. The pipe began to fill with thick, white smoke!

Part 4: The Smoke Chamber
Theme: Maximum Tension & The Countdown
Leo coughed, pulling his shirt up over his nose. The white smoke was thick and bitter. He couldn't breathe.
He was only two meters away from the exit grate that dropped into the hallway. But the smoke was making his eyes water, and his arms felt weak.
3 minutes left, his phone flashed through the haze.
He didn't have time to be scared. Leo threw his weight forward, ramming his shoulder directly into the exit grate.
Bang!
The screws snapped. The metal grate gave way, and Leo tumbled out of the vent, crashing hard onto the soft carpet of the upstairs hallway.
He gasped for fresh air, coughing heavily. He looked up.
The door to his dad's office at the end of the hall was wide open. A blue light flickered from inside. Someone was standing by the desk, holding a small silver flash drive.
It wasn't a hacker from the internet. It was a figure in a black jacket, plugging something into the main server.
Leo stood up, his legs shaking. He quietly picked up a heavy book from the hallway table. He had to stop whoever was inside before they downloaded the system.
He crept toward the open door, raised the book, and stepped into the room.

Part 5: The Final Face-Off
Theme: The Twist & Resolution
Leo rushed into the room, raising the book high. "Stop right there!"
The figure turned around quickly, dropping their hands.
The hood fell back. It was Maya, Leo’s tech-savvy classmate from the school robotics club. She was holding the silver flash drive, and she was grinning.
Leo froze, his arms dropping. "Maya? What are you doing? You hacked my house?"
Maya laughed and unplugged the flash drive. "Relax, Leo. It's not a real attack. It was a live-action puzzle test for the regional cybersecurity competition."
She pointed to the computer screen. On it was a live chat window with their school’s computer teacher.
"Your dad gave us permission to use his smart home lab for the test," Maya explained. "We needed someone with fast reflexes to test the escape protocol. You passed every single stage—the logical puzzle, the vent crawl, and the final confrontation."
Leo let out a long breath, dropping the book onto the desk. He couldn't decide whether to be furious or impressed.
"Next time," Leo said, wiping the dust off his jacket, "you should at least warn me before locking my door."
Maya smiled, tossing him the flash drive. "Well, Agent Leo, you survived. Welcome to the team."
